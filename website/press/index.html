PageDrop / Press
Press kit

PageDrop, in thirty seconds.

PageDrop is a free, open-source Android app that finds books and sends them to a jailbroken Kindle over Wi-Fi. No cable, no computer. It was built after Amazon cut older Kindles off from the Kindle Store, so those Kindles stay useful.

01 Descriptions

Copy these as they are

One line

PageDrop finds books on your Android phone and sends them to your jailbroken Kindle over Wi-Fi.

One paragraph

PageDrop is a free, open-source Android app for jailbroken Kindles running KOReader. Search public-domain libraries such as Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks and the Internet Archive, or your own OPDS catalogs, then tap send: PageDrop converts the book if it needs converting (or keeps it exactly as it is), fixes the title, author and cover, and copies it to the Kindle over Wi-Fi. A Kindle tab shows what's on the device, and a step-by-step guide gets a Kindle ready the first time. It was built after Amazon cut Kindles from 2012 and earlier off from the Kindle Store on May 20, 2026.

03 What it does

Features

  • Searches Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, the Internet Archive, Wikisource and your own OPDS catalogs.
  • Takes books shared from anywhere on the phone, or downloaded from a website inside the app.
  • Converts EPUB, PDF and text to MOBI when needed, with PDF reflow for text PDFs. Or keeps the file exactly as it is.
  • Fills in titles, authors and covers.
  • Sends over Wi-Fi (SFTP, through KOReader's SSH server) and refreshes the Kindle's library.
  • Shows what's on the Kindle and lets you delete books from it.
  • Walks first-time users through getting a Kindle ready, pointing to the Kindle Modding Wiki for the jailbreak.
